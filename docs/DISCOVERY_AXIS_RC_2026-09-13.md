RC's refuter strings use the word **"gate"** for two different things:

Arm definitions, all at ROW grain over the 36-row GFC subset, aggregation rule

- **STANDING** (A) - the refuter names a mechanised instrument that fires on its
  own trigger: a hook, CI, a named guard test, a suite, a named tool script.
- **CONVENED** (B) - the refuter names an adversarial, verifier, adjudicator or
  read-only pass, or a person-or-agent re-derivation.
- **NAME arm** - bare "gate" and "pre-commit gate" count as A, on the noun alone.
  This is the arm that maximally flatters REFUTATION of LW.
- **VERB arm** - classified by the act the refuter is said to have performed. An
  instrument GOES RED, FIRES, BREAKS, RUNS THE SUITE. A pass READS, MEASURES,
  NAMES, SHOWS, FINDS, RELOCATES, SEPARATES, RE-DERIVES.
- **CLEAR-ONLY arm** - the 11 bare-"gate" rows are dropped entirely and only the
  25 unambiguous rows are graded.
