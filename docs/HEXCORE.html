README

Amberstone

A live coaching companion for League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics. Reads the game's local data feed, watches the screen, and serves a second-screen dashboard with real-time advice: what to build, when to fight, who to ban in champ select. Personal project, private repo.

What it does

  • In-game coaching. Tracks gold, level, KDA, items, enemy build, and minimap from Riot's local feed, then turns it into short, situation-specific tips. Visual calls (wave state, fog inference, item-spike timing) go to an AI model; most ticks stay cheap and local.
  • Real math, not tier lists. A local build engine ("Daemon Slayer") computes true DPS, effective HP, ability burst, and healing for any champion x item x enemy. The AI coach reads those numbers so item advice fits your matchup.
  • Champion select. Suggests bans and picks from your own history, the enemy team, and patch trends, then writes runes into the client automatically.
  • Match history you own. ~3,000 of your matches with full timelines kept locally - no third-party scraping.
  • Modes: Summoner's Rift, ARAM, Arena, Brawl, and TFT.

How it works

A local Python service polls the game several times a second and decides whether the next tip comes from a quick local calculation or an AI call. The dashboard is a local web page opened in Chrome on a second monitor. The build engine runs as a separate local service: when the coach is about to suggest an item, it asks the engine for a ranked list with real damage numbers and drops that into its prompt. No cloud calls for the math.

Daemon Slayer build engine

Scores any champion x item x enemy combo with real game math, auto-picking one of six modes by role:

Role Scores
Carry (ADC)Auto-attack DPS
TankEffective HP vs enemy damage mix
BruiserPer-champion blend of DPS and EHP
MagePer-spell ability damage at cast cadence
AssassinBurst inside a combo window
EnchanterHealing and shielding throughput

A per-champion override registry handles unusual mechanics (Nidalee cougar form, Akali R recast, Zed shadow Q, Renekton Fury, and more). Roughly three-quarters of the roster is covered, growing in small batches. Coverage spans every purchasable item across all modes on the current patch.

Where it runs

Single-machine setup ("Legion"): coach, dashboard, build engine, vision, and the game all run on one host on a small private network. The dashboard is served locally over HTTPS and viewed in Chrome.

Status

In active development for over a year. The in-game loop is functionally complete across all five modes - vision, build engine, dashboard, champ select, and AI coaching are live and stable. Current work is polish: OCR-vs-AI region calibration, validating engine scores against match outcomes, filling the override registry, and iterating on the champ-select page.
