## Surface verdict (validated 2026-07-04, router-traced)

The operator sees exactly ONE lobby container out-of-game: the dedicated
full-page VIEW **`#view-lobby`** (web/index.html:821), rendered by
`_lobbyViewRefresh` (web/js/main.js:5221). The view-router auto-derives LCU
phase Lobby/Matchmaking/ReadyCheck -> the "lobby" view (main.js:704), and lobby
is "urgent" (_viewIsUrgent, main.js:713): the stale-manual clear (main.js:941-970)
force-clears any Home manual/hash the moment `auto === "lobby"`, so the operator
is always promoted to `#view-lobby` and cannot sit on Home while a lobby exists.

The inline card **`#lobby-overlay`** (index.html:368, renderLobbyPanel main.js:5989)
is superseded legacy: its content "moved into #view-lobby" (header.css:601-603),
it hard-gates to `body.dataset.view === "home"` (main.js:5999-6004), and is
CSS-hidden in lobby view (header.css:618). It is only reachable on a transient
phase=null blip while a lobby exists AND the operator is on Home - a near-dead
edge, NOT the steady state. (Router trace resolved an M1/M6 mapper disagreement:
M1 called it dead, M6 called it blip-reachable; both partly right.)

`?overlay=1` HUD is irrelevant to lobby (hard-pins active-match, main.js:921-924).
Primary viewport = companion window (920x1280 portrait primary, 1920 secondary),
same as home.
