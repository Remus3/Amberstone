## Ambiguities I resolved

Each entry quotes the clause I found insufficient and states the resolution I
imposed. These are places the convention did not determine its own application.

**1. GATE-FIRED-CAUGHT and GATE-ABSENT are on different axes and the convention
never says whether they co-apply.** Section 3 pins the judgement time frame at
"information available AT THE MOMENT THE DEFECT WAS WRITTEN", but 3.4 defines
GATE-FIRED-CAUGHT entirely at refutation time - "A standing check DID fire,
correctly, its output was ACTED ON, and that is why the claim was refuted."
Almost every row in this block was caught by something; on the literal reading of
3.2 a DIRECTED pass is not a mechanised instrument, so GATE-ABSENT is
simultaneously true of nearly every GATE-FIRED-CAUGHT row. 3.9's admission rule
("a value enters the set only where the ROW'S OWN TEXT independently grounds it")
does not settle it, because the row's text grounds both. **My resolution:**
GATE-FIRED-CAUGHT is taken ALONE unless the row independently grounds a distinct
prevention failure (a named vacuous or wrong-scoped guard, a named substituted
measurement, or a named rule). Without this rule the modal set in this block
becomes two values and the SPLIT/multi-value counts that 3.9 and weakness 5 ask
readers to police become uninterpretable. This is the single largest judgement in
my pass and a different reader applying the same document could reasonably admit
both on most rows.

**2. Section 3.8's two pinned conditions conflict with each other, and I had to
pick which one is operative.** Clause 1 is "Residual ordering. ADVERSARY is
checked LAST. If any other value's definition is satisfied, that value is taken."
Clause 2 is the SHAPE test, which reserves ADVERSARY for reasoning errors. But
3.2's constructibility test is permissive enough that a plausible instrument can
be named for almost any reasoning error after the fact, so clause 1 read strictly
empties ADVERSARY entirely. **My resolution:** clause 2 is operative where they
conflict, because 3.8's own worked boundary ("A claim that a measurement supports
a conclusion it does not support ... is ADVERSARY, not GATE-ABSENT, however
mechanical the underlying measurement was") can only be true if shape beats
ordering. Rows chunk2-14, chunk2-24 and chunk2-30 through chunk2-33 turn on this.

**3. 3.2's "using only what was knowable before the defect's content was known"
is not operable as written.** Every candidate predicate I wrote had to be phrased
in some vocabulary, and the vocabulary usually came from the defect. "A check
asserting the count is 173" is barred by the explicit example, but "a check that
the filed count equals the re-derived count" is the same instrument with the
answer factored out, and the clause does not say which phrasing governs. **My
resolution:** I allowed answer-free phrasings (consistency, re-derivation, and
agreement checks) as constructible and barred only predicates that must embed the
correct value or the defect's own framing. This is the clause that decides
GATE-ABSENT versus ADVERSARY on thin rows, and RC has relocated rather than
closed the gap it criticised: the eight values are now defined, but the
CONSTRUCTIBILITY predicate that arbitrates between two of them is not.

**4. 3.4's BROAD standing-check reading does not say what makes a pass
"standing" when the row names only a role.** The definition requires "a NAMED
instrument WITH A FIRING VERB which a STANDING RULE REQUIRED to run on this class
of work", and its boundary says "If the row's text does not identify WHICH
instrument fired, the value cannot be assigned." Refuters in this block include
"the merger", "the build", "the slice", "the cycle audit", "this cycle's prose
audit", "the RM-407 audit", "an adversarial slice" and "three passes". Nothing in
the document separates a role from an instrument. **My resolution:** a pass owed
BEFORE the claim could stand (verifier, adjudicator, adversarial pass, cycle or
prose audit, a named guard or hook) is standing; the work itself doing its own
job (the build, the slice, the merger inspecting, an item's own audit such as
"the RM-407 audit") is not. This single line moved eight rows between
GATE-FIRED-CAUGHT and GATE-ABSENT/ADVERSARY, and it is my invention, not RC's.

**5. 3.5 condition 2 - "it was not wrong about the thing it measured" - is
undecidable when the instrument is a HEURISTIC.** chunk2-09's receiver-name
heuristic both correctly reported its own matches and was a bad detector of root
walks; both readings fit the clause. **My resolution:** correctness is judged
against the instrument's literal predicate, never against the believed quantity,
otherwise every proxy collapses into "the instrument was wrong" and the value can
never be assigned.

**6. 3.5 also does not say whether PROXY-MEASURE DISPLACES an
otherwise-applicable GATE-ABSENT.** The clause only handles the reverse ("If the
row says a measurement was believed too strongly but names no second quantity
... prevention is scored on the defect's own merits, typically GATE-ABSENT or
ADVERSARY"). **My resolution:** where PROXY-MEASURE applies I take it alone
against GATE-ABSENT, since 4.3 declines a total precedence order and this is the
only direction the text hints at. This matters because PROXY-MEASURE is
OUT-FAMILY, so the choice moves the family answer, not just the set.

**7. Section 6.1's cross-row link rule cannot be executed under a single
file-order pass.** It says "A forward link established by a DIFFERENT ROW IN THE
SAME CORPUS does count", which requires reading later rows before finalising
earlier ones. chunk2-36 refutes the probe that partly remedied chunk2-35, and
chunk3-22 and chunk3-23 stand in the same relation to earlier CRLF work. I scored
every `fix_chain` at the 6.1 floor of 0 and flagged the affected rows rather than
revising. The convention's floor rule absorbs this, but the result is that
`fix_chain` in this block carries no information at all, which is worth saying
plainly.

**8. 5.1's "committed, filed, or sitting in a tracked artifact" does not resolve
for an RM row or a directive.** A row filed under an RM id is tracked by
construction, but 5.2(a) tells me to default to FRESH absent evidence of
durability in the row's own text, and the rows that merely say "RM-402's filed
figures" do not state where the filing lives. **My resolution:** an RM-numbered
row, a directive consumed by a LATER cycle, a hand-off note, a docstring and
committed code are INHERITED; a draft, a prediction, a slice report, a
same-session dispatch and in-chat statements to the operator are FRESH. RC's own
5.1 sub-rules pin the directive case but not the filed-row case.

**9. 5.2(b)'s positive tests exclude a common shape and the convention has no
home for it.** A claim re-derived over a DIFFERENT corpus than the one it was
filed against (chunk2-43, filed at 90 or 91 transcripts, re-derived at 93) fails
the DECAYED test (no time-index of a change) and the BORN-WRONG test (the
re-derivation does not show it was wrong at filing). I assigned UNKNOWN as the
document requires, but weakness 7 predicts the consequence and this block
supplies it: UNKNOWN here is not "the record is silent", it is "the record is
loud and the four tests do not fit".

**10. 3.1's VACUOUS reason has no partial case.** chunk3-23's guard was green
under one demonstrated mutation and presumably red under others. "it ran and
measured NOTHING" is an absolute, and a partially insensitive guard satisfies
neither it nor the alternatives. I assigned GATE-EXISTING with VACUOUS anyway,
which overstates the guard's emptiness.

**11. The output format I was given has no `prevention_why` field**, which 3.1
makes mandatory on GATE-EXISTING. I recorded it in the `note` line for chunk3-16
and chunk3-23.

**12. 1.3's boundary is stated but this pass is forbidden to act on it.**
chunk2-31 and chunk2-32 are, by chunk2-32's own words, "the same sufficiency
assumption, in its second failing case" - one claim with two failing instances,
which 1.3 calls ONE event ("a general assertion plus its own instance, is ONE
event"). I scored both as filed. The convention's individuation rule and a
per-row scoring task are not compatible without a separate individuation pass.
